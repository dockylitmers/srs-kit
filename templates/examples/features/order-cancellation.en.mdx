## Purpose

Customers cancel a paid order themselves from the order detail screen in the app and get a refund to the card they paid with. Orders that customers cannot cancel themselves are refunded by an admin from the admin console.

## In scope

- Customer cancels the whole order (order detail screen in the app)
- Refund by cancelling the card payment
- Returning coupons and points
- Admin refunds (orders the customer cannot cancel)
- Notification when a cancellation or refund completes (app push, email)

## Not in this release

- Partial cancellation: customers can only cancel the whole order. Refunds for part of an order are admin refunds.
- Bank transfer or cash refunds: payments are card only, so refunds are card payment cancellations only.
- Cancellation fees: none in this release.
- A statistics screen for cancellation reasons

## Rules

| ID | Rule | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAN-01 | If the order status is `결제 완료` (paid), the customer can cancel the whole order until 23:59 KST on day 7, counting the payment date as day 1. Otherwise the order detail screen shows "고객센터로 문의해 주세요" (please contact customer support) instead of the cancel button. | SRC-002, SRC-009 |
| CAN-02 | If the order status is `발급 완료` (issued), the customer cannot cancel it and only an admin can refund it. Otherwise CAN-01 applies. | SRC-002 |
| CAN-03 | The refund amount is the full amount the customer paid by card. Coupon discounts and points used are not part of the refund amount; they are returned under CAN-04 and CAN-05. | SRC-003 |
| CAN-04 | If the coupon is still valid at cancellation, it is returned with its original expiry. Otherwise it is not returned. | SRC-003 |
| CAN-05 | All points used are returned at cancellation. If the points have expired, they can be used until 23:59 on day (proposed) 30, counting the return date as day 1. Otherwise they keep their original expiry. | SRC-005 |
| CAN-06 | After the cancellation completes, the completion screen shows "카드사에 따라 영업일 3~7일 안에 환불됩니다" (refunds take 3 to 7 business days depending on the card issuer). | SRC-004 |
| CAN-07 | If the admin has the `운영자` (operator) or `최고 관리자` (super admin) role, they can refund. Otherwise the refund button is hidden. A refund records a reason chosen from a list, who processed it, and the time. | SRC-006 |
| CAN-08 | When a cancellation or refund completes, the customer is notified by app push and email. If the customer has turned off app push, only email is sent. Otherwise both are sent. | SRC-007 |

### Status transitions

| Current status | Who | Action | Next status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `결제 완료` (paid) | Customer (within the CAN-01 deadline) | Cancel | `취소 완료` (cancelled) |
| `결제 완료` (paid) | Admin | Refund | `환불 완료` (refunded) |
| `발급 완료` (issued) | Admin | Refund | `환불 완료` (refunded) |
| `발급 완료` (issued) | Customer | Cancel | Not allowed |
| `취소 완료`, `환불 완료` | Anyone | Cancel, refund | Not allowed |

## Examples

| Example | Rule | Given | When | Then |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | CAN-01 | Paid 10/1 14:00, `결제 완료` | Customer cancels at 10/7 23:59 | The order is cancelled |
| E2 | CAN-01 | Paid 10/1 14:00, `결제 완료` | Customer opens the order detail at 10/8 00:00 | No cancel button; "고객센터로 문의해 주세요" is shown |
| E3 | CAN-01 | Paid 10/1 23:59, `결제 완료` | Customer cancels at 10/7 23:59 | The order is cancelled (the payment date is day 1 regardless of the time) |
| E4 | CAN-02 | `발급 완료` | Customer opens the order detail | No cancel button |
| E5 | CAN-03, CAN-04, CAN-05 | Item 30,000 KRW, coupon 5,000 KRW off (still valid), 2,000 points used, 23,000 KRW paid by card | Customer cancels | 23,000 KRW refunded to the card; the coupon and 2,000 points are returned |
| E6 | CAN-04 | Coupon valid until 10/5 23:59 | Customer cancels on 10/6 | The coupon is not returned |
| E7 | CAN-05 | Points valid until 10/5 23:59 | Customer cancels on 10/6 | 2,000 points are returned and can be used until (proposed) 11/4 23:59 |
| E8 | CAN-06 | Order paid by card | Customer cancels | The completion screen shows "카드사에 따라 영업일 3~7일 안에 환불됩니다" |
| E9 | CAN-07 | Admin with the `고객 지원` (customer support) role | Opens the order detail | No refund button |
